# Pets

Now and then a pet comes to visit your window: a cat, a dog, a fox, a slime, a ghost or a chick. It walks around, sits, naps and peeks in from the edges. After a while it heads off.

## Playing with them

- **Click** a pet to pet it. It says something back.
- **Drag** it to carry it. It dangles by the scruff.
- **Fling** it and let go to throw it. It tumbles, bounces off the walls, ceiling and floor, and lands dizzy. Click it in mid-air to catch it.
- Throw the same pet hard twice within 25 seconds, and it faints and vanishes in a puff.

## Calling them

These are commands (**Ctrl+Shift+P**):

- **Call a Pet Now**: one comes at once.
- **A Pet Peeks In**: one peeks over an edge of the window.
- **Send the Pets Away**.
- **Turn Pets On or Off**.

## Settings > Pets

- **Pets visit now and then**: on or off.
- Each pet's **skin**: pick one, or **Any** for a different one each visit. Every pet has several (the cat: orange tabby, black, white, grey tabby, cream and lilac).
- **Seconds a visit lasts** (30 to start with).
- **Outline**: black, or soft (a dark shade of the pet's own colour).
- **When you pick one up**: it dangles and swings, or keeps walking in the air.
- **Thrown twice, they faint**: switch it off if you like.

**Settings > Look & motion > Reduce motion** makes the pets sit still.

## More pets and skins

The [Marketplace](extensions.md) has pets and skin packs. **Pet Pack** brings a frog, a penguin and a bunny; **Pet Pack Skins** gives them more looks; **Cat Skins**, **Dog Skins** and **Fox Skins** give Aether's own pets more. Install one, and it shows in Settings > Pets with the others. (Skin packs need Aether 1.9.28 or newer.) You can also make your own pet, drawn in code or from a sprite sheet: see [Making extensions](API.md#aether-pets-pets-of-your-own).
