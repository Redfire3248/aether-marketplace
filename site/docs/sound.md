# Sound Studio

**Create > Open Sound Studio**, or **SFX** in the dock. A switch at the top goes between **Music** and **Sound effects**. **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y** undo and redo, and only one song or sound plays at a time.

## Music

Songs are made of tracks, like a small music studio.

- **Instruments**: leads, plucks, chip arps, bass, saw bass, pads, strings, bells, organ, flute, a drum kit, and any sound effect or WAV, played at any pitch.
- **Piano roll** for each track: click to add a note, drag to make it longer or move it, **Ctrl+D** copies.
- An overview of every track, **mute** and **solo**, volume and pan, tempo, swing, reverb and loop.
- **Compose** writes a new song in a style: Adventure, Chiptune, Dungeon, Cave, Boss fight or Calm.
- **Record** your voice (or anything) onto a track of its own.

Songs save as `.song` files, export as WAV, and play in games as music: `game.music("theme")`. A looping song has no gap.

## Sound effects

- Up to **four layers** in one sound, with nine waveforms (pulse, soft noise, rumble, metallic and more), melodies and FM.
- Filters, distortion, bit crushing, echo and reverb.
- **64 presets** for platformers, action, RPGs, menus, sci-fi, horror, nature and retro games.
- **Record your voice** as a layer: pitch it up or down, speed it up, play it backwards, filter it and crunch it. Or bring in a .wav, .mp3 or .ogg.

Sounds save as `.sfx` files you can keep changing. **Add to game** puts the WAV in your game's `assets/sounds`.

## Playing sounds

Open any sound file and Aether's player shows: play and stop, a waveform to click or drag through, the volume and Loop. **Edit in Sound Studio** opens it on a track of its own.
