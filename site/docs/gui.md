# GUI Studio

GUI Studio (**GUI** in the dock) is for game menus, HUDs and buttons, a bit like Figma. When it looks right, press **Add to game**: it goes into the open scene as a screen. Press it again to update that screen. **Design in GUI Studio** on a screen opens it back.

## Shapes and layers

- **Frames** that hold other layers and can cut them to their shape, and **groups** (**Ctrl+G**).
- **Auto layout**: stack layers down, across or in a grid, with a gap, padding and alignment.
- Rectangles with round or cut corners (each corner on its own), ellipses, pills, triangles, diamonds, polygons, stars, hearts and icons.
- **The pen tool**: click for corners, drag for curves, click the first point to close. Double-click a shape to move its points.
- **Text** in every font on your PC, and the fonts that come with Aether.
- New layers stay on top. Hold **Ctrl** to drop one into a frame.

## Fills, strokes and effects

- Stack as many **fills** as you like: solid, linear, radial, a picture, or patterns (stripes, dots, checker, grid, zigzag, noise) that can fade out.
- Several **strokes**, inside, centred or outside.
- **Effects**: drop shadow (soft or hard), inner shadow, glow, and a 3D lip for raised buttons.
- A colour picker with an eyedropper, hex, RGB and HSB, and the colours already on the page. **Selection colours** change a colour everywhere it is used.

## Layout tools

Snapping to the edges and middles of everything, guides, alignment, even spacing, flip, front and back, **Flatten** into one picture, and PNG export at 1x to 4x. Pick several layers to change them together.

## Animate

Keyframe animations for **Hover**, **Click**, **Appear** and **Loop**, from templates (grow, lift, pop, wiggle, bounce, shake, jelly, fade, slide in, float, pulse, spin, heartbeat and more), on a timeline you can scrub.

## Ready-made pieces

Game buttons with stripes, dots, an outline and a lip, a close button, panels and bars, a glass card, a neon button, title text, a coin and a star badge.

## Shortcuts

From Figma: **Ctrl+D** duplicate, **Ctrl+E** flatten, **Ctrl+Shift+H** hide, **Ctrl+Shift+L** lock, **Ctrl+R** or **F2** rename, **Ctrl+Alt+C** / **V** copy and paste the look, **Alt+A / D / W / S / H / V** align, **Shift+0 / 1 / 2** zoom to 100%, fit and the selection.
