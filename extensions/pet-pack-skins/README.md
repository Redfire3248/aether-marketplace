# Pet Pack Skins

More looks for the Pet Pack: a poison blue and a golden frog, an emperor and a pink penguin, a brown and a grey bunny.

- **Frog**: Poison blue, Golden.
- **Penguin**: Emperor, Pink.
- **Bunny**: Brown, Grey.

After installing, open **Settings > Pets** and pick a skin for the frog, the penguin or the bunny, or leave it on **Any** to see a different one each visit. It needs **Pet Pack** installed too: the skins wait for its pets and show as soon as they are there.

Needs Aether 1.9.27 or newer.
