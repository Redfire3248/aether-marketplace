# Pet Pack

Three more pets for Aether: a frog, a penguin and a bunny. Their other looks are in Pet Pack Skins.

Adds a **Frog**, a **Penguin** and a **Bunny**. They visit like Aether's own pets, and you can pet, carry and throw them. Find them in **Settings > Pets**, where you can call them and peek them in.

For more looks (a poison blue or golden frog, an emperor or pink penguin, a brown or grey bunny), install **Pet Pack Skins** too.
